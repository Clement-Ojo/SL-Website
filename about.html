The people behind the prototype

Building healthcare
that listens to everyone.

SignLanguageAI is an early-stage digital-health accessibility startup being developed by a student-led team at the University of Ibadan.

Our mission

Make direct communication a more natural part of care.

We are building tools that help Deaf and hard-of-hearing patients communicate more directly with healthcare professionals, with clarity, dignity and agency.

Our vision

A future where access is designed in, not added later.

Healthcare should meet people in the ways they communicate. We believe technology can help close gaps while keeping people and professional judgement at the centre.

Why SignLanguageAI

The product is the point.

We are focused on a specific, real problem: the communication gap between Deaf patients and healthcare professionals. Our work is not a replacement for doctors, nurses or professional interpreters. It is an attempt to create a more accessible communication layer around the consultation.
