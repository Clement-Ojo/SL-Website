01 / Six steps

From communication to continuity.

01Patient communicates

The patient shares symptoms, context and questions through sign language.

02System interprets

AI-assisted interpretation helps translate visual communication into usable information.

03Professional responds

The healthcare professional can communicate back, keeping the dialogue two-way.

04Information is structured

Key details are organized so the clinical conversation is easier to follow.

05Summary created

A consultation summary captures what was shared for review and confirmation.

06Care ID supports continuity

The patient can choose to carry relevant context into future care.
