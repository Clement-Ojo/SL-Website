Why it matters

Communication is part of care.

People with disabilities represent a significant proportion of Nigeria’s population, yet many continue to face discrimination, exclusion and barriers to essential services, including healthcare.

15%
Estimated share of Nigeria’s population living with disability.World Bank / National Commission for Persons with Disabilities context
01

Communication barriers

When patient and provider cannot communicate directly, care starts with a gap.

02

Limited accessibility

Healthcare environments are not always designed for every way of communicating.

03

Informal interpretation

Patients may depend on relatives or untrained intermediaries to be understood.

04

Loss of clinical detail

Nuance can be lost when symptoms pass through multiple people.

05

Reduced participation

Patients deserve to ask, understand and decide as active participants in their care.

06

Continuity challenges

Important context should not disappear between one visit and the next.

The problem is not the patient’s ability to communicate. It is the communication gap within the healthcare system. SignLanguageAI is being built to help close that gap.
